### Chinese Service { .card-title }

Whether you are looking for a caring spiritual home, searching for the meaning of life, or simply want to worship in your heart's language, we warmly welcome you to our Chinese Service.

​Our community is a vibrant, multi-generational family where traditional heritage meets dynamic faith. We gather every week to worship God passionately, listen to life-transforming messages from His Word, build deep, supportive friendships and grow in faith together. 

We encourage you to join the church fellowship after service to truly experience a multi-language church community.
