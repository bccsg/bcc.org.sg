### Monthly Prayer Meeting { .card-title }

Our Monthly Sunday Prayer Meetings are a special time for our church family to come together in unity before the service begins. Held one hour before our Sunday service, this dedicated hour creates space for us to seek God together, align our hearts, and prepare spiritually for what He desires to do among us.

As a praying church, prayer is not simply something we do—it is part of who we are. We believe that hosting the Holy Spirit is in our DNA, and these gatherings allow us to intentionally welcome His presence, listen for His leading, and intercede for our church, community, and the world. Join us on the 4th Sunday of every month to seek God's face.
